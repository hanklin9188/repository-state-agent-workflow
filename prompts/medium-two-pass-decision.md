# Medium Two-Pass Decision Prompt

```text
Use Medium reasoning and split this decision into two explicit passes.

Pass A — Evidence decomposition:
- read AGENTS.md, ACTIVE.md, the decision ticket, and exact evidence;
- separate observed facts from inference;
- list competing options, constraints, missing evidence, and human values;
- write a compact decision checkpoint.

Pass B — Decision synthesis:
- reread the checkpoint and governing constraints;
- choose or narrow the architecture;
- state trade-offs and unresolved assumptions;
- create a decision/proposal artifact;
- update ACTIVE.md and stop.

Do not implement or execute the decision in this session.
```
